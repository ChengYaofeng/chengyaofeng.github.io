Transparent objects often cause depth cameras to produce incomplete or invalid measurements, reducing the reliability of robotic grasping. ReMake addresses this problem with a depth completion framework guided by instance masks and monocular depth estimation.

The instance mask explicitly separates transparent and non-transparent regions, helping the model focus on depth estimation in transparent areas. Monocular depth estimation supplies context between the object and its surroundings. The framework is evaluated on benchmark datasets and real-world robotic grasping experiments.

Code and videos are available on the [project page](https://chengyaofeng.github.io/ReMake.github.io/).
